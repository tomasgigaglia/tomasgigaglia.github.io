From worn part to new part

How a reverse engineering job usually runs, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Capture the existing part with a 3D scanner, or measure it by hand.

  2. 02

    Model

    Rebuild it as a clean parametric solid in Inventor, Solid Edge or SolidWorks.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Produce detail and assembly drawings with the tolerances the workshop needs.

  4. 04

    Make

    Print a prototype in PLA, ASA or ABS, or hand it over for machining or fabrication.
