Part reconstruction from 3D scan

When a part is worn, broken or no longer made, I scan it, clean up the mesh and rebuild it as a parametric model, designing out the wear so the new part fits the way the original was meant to.

From worn part to new part

How a reverse engineering job usually runs, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Capture the existing part with a 3D scanner, or measure it by hand.

  2. 02

    Model

    Rebuild it as a clean parametric solid in Inventor, Solid Edge, SolidWorks or Fusion 360.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Detail and assembly drawings with the fits, tolerances and surface finishes the workshop needs.

  4. 04

    Make

    Print a prototype in PLA, ASA or ABS to check fit, then hand over for machining or fabrication.
